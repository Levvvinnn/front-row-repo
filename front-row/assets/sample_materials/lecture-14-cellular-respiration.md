# Lecture 14: Cellular Respiration

## Glycolysis
- Takes place in the cytoplasm
- One glucose becomes two pyruvate
- Net yield: 2 ATP, 2 NADH

## Pyruvate oxidation
- Pyruvate enters the mitochondria and becomes acetyl CoA

## The Krebs cycle (citric acid cycle)
- Takes place in the mitochondrial matrix
- Runs twice per glucose
- Products per glucose: 6 NADH, 2 FADH2, 2 ATP, 4 CO2

## Electron transport chain
- Located on the inner mitochondrial membrane
- Pumps protons into the intermembrane space
- Oxygen is the final electron acceptor
- ATP synthase makes ATP by chemiosmosis

## Fermentation
- Happens without oxygen
- Lactic acid fermentation and alcohol fermentation
