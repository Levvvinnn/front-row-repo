# Front Row 🎟️

A Cubbie Skill for the Cubbie Hackathon (Track 1).

Your professor tells you what's on the exam. You just weren't listening.

Record your lecture and send it to Cubbie. Front Row catches every time your professor says "this will be on
the exam", "know this" or "people always get this wrong", matches each hint to your Canvas slides, flags the
ones that are only said in lecture, and sends you the full list a week before the exam.

## Install

Text this link to Cubbie:

https://github.com/OWNER/REPO/tree/main/front-row

## How to use it

1. Put your phone on the desk and record the lecture with Voice Memos (it keeps recording with the screen off).
2. After class, share the recording to Cubbie. Or send the Voice Memos transcript as text.
3. Get your hints for that lecture. A week before the exam, get the whole list as a PDF.

Check each class's syllabus allows recording, and keep recordings for your own studying.

## What it uses

Only Cubbie's built-in abilities: transcribing voice notes and videos, Canvas (if connected), memory, scheduled
check-ins and documents. No scripts and no keys.
